Decisions for the owner
  1. [[ links a canvas; # after it links a heading, database or row. A pasted host URL for a canvas becomes the same link. No @ for canvases (that stays for people).
  2. A link always shows the canvas's current title from the host. The label saved in the document is used only by exports and older editors.
  3. “Linked from” sits at the end of every canvas and in the side panel, one group per canvas, newest edit first, with the sentence around each link.
  4. Counts and lists include only canvases the reader can open. There is no “and 3 more you can't see”.
  5. Excerpts are made when the panel opens and are not stored (roadmap question 1 asks whether to store a short encrypted excerpt instead).
  6. Unlinked mentions come from the organisation's search; the section is hidden when the host has none. Folded when there are more than 5.
  7. Link on a mention edits the other canvas as you, needs edit access there, and is one undo step there. Link all links only the canvases you can edit.
  8. The preview opens after 400 ms of hover or with Space on a focused link, never on focus alone.
  9. The “Linked from” chip shows on headings, databases, boards and rows that have backlinks; not on every paragraph.
  10. Phone: a section at the end, a sheet from the top bar, long-press for the preview.

Backlinks and linked references

Prototype for review (v0.5, docs/38 §2, ADR-081). Canvases and blocks can be linked with [[, and every canvas shows what links to it, with context, plus mentions of its name that are not links yet. The server keeps only which canvas links to which (ids, no text); titles and excerpts are read from the host with the reader's access each time, so nobody sees a link from a canvas they cannot open. Sample content: Acme Docs, Engineering space, the “SSO rollout plan” canvas. Frames 2a and 3a are live.

1 · Linking a canvas or a block LINK-01 · ADR-081

Type [[ to link another canvas. Results come from the host's search and the canvases you opened recently, so you only ever see canvases you can open. Add # to link a heading, a database or a row inside it. A pasted host URL for a canvas becomes the same kind of link.

2 · “Linked from” and unlinked mentions LINK-03 · LINK-04 · LINK-05

At the end of every canvas (and in the side panel, 2b), the canvases that link here, grouped by canvas, each with the sentence around the link. Below, canvases that mention this one by name without linking it. Frame 2a is live: filter by kind or block, fold a group, open a reference, or press Link on a mention.

3 · Hover preview and link states LINK-04 · ADR-072

Resting on a canvas or block link for 400 ms (or focusing it and pressing Space) shows a preview: title, place, last edit, and the first lines of the canvas or the linked block, read through the host with your role. Frame 3a is live: hover or focus the links.

4 · Phone below 45em

“Linked from” is a section at the end of the canvas and a sheet from the top bar. Long-press a link for its preview as a sheet. The [[ picker sits above the keyboard.

5 · Empty, off and limited [host.flags] links

With [host.flags] links off, nothing on this page appears: [[ inserts an ordinary link through the host's picker as in 0.4, and pasted URLs stay plain links.

6 · Keyboard and access

Every link is a real link (Tab, Enter). The panel is a list of groups; each group is a disclosure button followed by its references, each a link to that place in the source canvas.
