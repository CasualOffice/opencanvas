Decisions for the owner
  1. Local graph opens at depth 1 (this canvas and everything one link away); the slider goes to 3.
  2. Block nodes are off by default. "Show blocks" adds the headings, databases and rows that other canvases link to.
  3. Nodes are coloured by space by default (the host's top-level folder or team); Updated and Owner are the alternatives.
  4. The full graph draws at most 2,000 canvases, the most linked ones the viewer can open, with a banner when it cuts (6d).
  5. Canvases the viewer cannot open are left out with no placeholder and no count, and links through them are cut (ADR-082).
  6. Orphans (canvases with no links) are shown in the full graph; one switch hides them.
  7. Pinned positions are per person, kept in the browser, never in the document or on the server.
  8. On a phone the list is the default; the graph is one tap away.
  9. Enter or a double-click opens the canvas through the host (the existing open-canvas message); the graph never opens a canvas itself.

Knowledge graph

Prototype for review (v0.5). The knowledge graph draws the canvases you can open as nodes and the links between them as lines, from the server's link index (ADR-081) through the graph API (ADR-082). It has two entry points: a local graph beside the canvas you are in, and a full graph of the workspace. Everything is also available as a list.

Frames 1a, 1b, 2a, 2b, 3a and 5a are live: the layout is a small force simulation in plain JavaScript (no library) that settles and stops. Hover, click, drag, zoom, the filters, the depth slider and the keyboard all work. Sample workspace: Acme Docs, 33 canvases in five spaces.

1 · The graph GRAPH-03 · ADR-082

Every canvas you can open is a circle, sized by how many links it has and coloured by its space. Lines are links between canvases: solid for a link, dashed for a live block shown in another canvas, dotted for a database relation across canvases. The edges come from the server's link index (ADR-081); the host decides which canvases you can open at the moment you look.

2 · Filters and search GRAPH-03

Filters change what is drawn; the layout keeps the canvases that stay where they were, so the picture does not jump. Search does not remove anything: it highlights matches and dims the rest, so you can still see where a match sits.

3 · Hover preview and selection GRAPH-03

Hovering shows a card and lights up the neighbours; the arrows on the lit lines show which way each link goes. Clicking keeps the card open with actions.

4 · List view and keyboard GRAPH-03 · ACC

The graph is a picture; the list is the same data as a table, for screen readers, for people who prefer it, and for small screens. Graph | List in the toolbar switches (or press L in the graph). Filters and search apply to both.

5 · Phone GRAPH-03

Below 45em the list is the default, with a toggle to the graph. The graph fills the screen; tapping a canvas opens a sheet instead of a hover card. Every target is at least 44 px.
