Database Timeline and Gantt

Prototype for review (v0.4, "advanced databases"). Two new database views: Timeline, a Gantt chart over any database with a date field, and Workload, people against weeks. Both are views of the same rows, like Table and Board, so filters, sorts, groups and live editing work the same way.

Frame 1 is live: drag a bar to move it, drag its right edge to change its length, use the zoom and Critical path controls, or focus a bar and use the arrow keys. Moving "API freeze" past its dependents shows the conflict message from frame 2b.

1 · Timeline view

Rows on the left, bars across weeks on the right. Group by any select field (here Phase). Milestones are diamonds; dependencies are arrows from a task's end to the next task's start. A red line marks today.

2 · Dependencies and editing

Dependencies are a relation field on the same database ("Blocked by"). Drawing an arrow fills it in; editing the field draws the arrow. Nothing moves automatically unless you say so.

3 · Setting up a timeline

Adding a Timeline view picks sensible fields on its own: the first two date fields, or one date field plus a duration. The settings panel lets you change them.

4 · Workload view

People down the side, weeks across. Each cell adds the Estimate (hours) of that person's tasks that overlap the week, spread over working days, against their weekly capacity (default 40, set per person in the view). Click a cell to see the tasks and drag one to someone else.

5 · Phone

A Gantt chart does not fit a phone, so below 45em the Timeline becomes a list by week with a small bar for each row's span. Tapping a row opens it; long-press gives the bar menu as a sheet.

7 · Keyboard and access

The left column is a grid (one Tab stop). Each bar is a focusable button named with its row, dates and dependencies, for example "API freeze, Dec 14 to Dec 18, blocks SDK docs and Partner beta". Every change is also possible in the row's fields, so nothing depends on dragging.
