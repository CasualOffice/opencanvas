Mattermost plugin screens

Every screen and state from integrations/mattermost/UI-SCREENS.md, drawn at real sizes (desktop 1280 px, phone 390 px in a bezel; a few panel and post-list crops at their own size). Nothing here is built; these are for approval before any UI code (ADR-011).

Who draws what. Mattermost chrome (global header, channel sidebar, center channel, App Bar, right-hand panel, modals, System Console, Desktop app, mobile app) is recreated in Mattermost 12's own style: Denim in light, Onyx in dark. Our card, panel header, dialog and empty states follow the user's Mattermost theme. Everything inside a canvas is OpenCanvas, using its approved tokens and patterns.

Two issues from the real-host run are designed here: the template picker in the narrow panel (S2e to S2g) and the Welcome checklist over the Open button (S1h, S1i).

Decisions for the owner are called out in the notes: skip the picker for new canvases (S2f, recommended) or open them expanded (S2g); presence only in the editor bar (S2); Download shown greyed or hidden (S1b); Open in new window in the Desktop app (S7b); the phone browser link (S8b, S8c).

S1 · Post card custom_opencanvas

The card is a Mattermost post, drawn in the user's Mattermost theme. Actions sit on the right of the title row, not under the text: this keeps Open clear of Mattermost's first-run checklist (S1h, S1i) and keeps the card short in a busy channel. Clicking anywhere on the card also opens it.

S1W · First-run Welcome checklist onboarding task list

Seen in the real-host run: on a narrow window Mattermost's first-run "Welcome to Mattermost" checklist floats over the bottom-left of the post list, exactly where today's placeholder card puts Open. We cannot move Mattermost's checklist, so the card moves its buttons to the right of the title row.

S2 · Canvas panel and expanded view registerRightHandSidebarComponent

The canvas opens in Mattermost's right-hand panel (400 px), and Mattermost's own expand turns it into the wide view. The panel header is ours but drawn in Mattermost's style: the canvas title and channel, then Canvases, Open in new window, Expand and Close. People in the canvas are shown once, in the editor's own bar, because it is live there. Everything below the header is OpenCanvas, in its compact layout.

S3 · Create dialog channel header button · /canvas new

A Mattermost modal in the user's theme. Title is optional; the kind is two big choices. The note says who can edit and where it is stored, in plain words. Enter submits. /canvas new [title] skips the dialog and creates a blank canvas directly.

S4 · Channel canvases list RHS view

Opened from the App Bar's Channel canvases button, or from the grid button in a canvas panel's header. Newest first, with the live dot when people are in a canvas. Clicking a row opens it in the same panel (S2). Guests without access do not see the App Bar button at all.

S5 · Slash commands and replies /canvas

Command replies are Mattermost ephemeral posts ("Only visible to you"), from the OpenCanvas bot. Plain text, one or two sentences, and always the next step.

S6 · System Console settings plugin.json settings_schema

Mattermost draws this page from plugin.json; we only write the labels and help text. A Test connection button is a candidate for later.

S7 · Desktop app Mattermost Desktop

The Desktop app runs the same web app inside its own window. Two differences: it may refuse clipboard writes from the canvas frame, and "Open in new window" can only open the system browser.

S8 · Mattermost mobile apps no plugin UI

The phone apps run no plugin UI. Today the card is the post's text. A proposed later step adds a link that opens the canvas in the phone's browser. Needs a decision before it is built.
