What is OpenCanvas?

OpenCanvas, by CasualOffice, is the open-source collaborative workspace engine for your application: a real-time collaborative canvas that other applications embed. One document holds documents, spreadsheets, databases, whiteboards and diagrams, with live cursors and comments. The host application keeps the users, the permissions and the file; OpenCanvas does the editing, merges every change live and saves the file back to the host. {{claim:source|The open-source collaborative workspace engine for your application.|README.md}}
