Brand

The OpenCanvas logos, and how to name OpenCanvas in a product that embeds it. Credit is welcome and never required.

Logos

Use the files as they are: don't recolour, stretch, crop or add effects. Leave clear space of at least a quarter of the logo's height around it.

Wording

  • Preferred: Powered by OpenCanvas
  • Alternative: Built with OpenCanvas
  • Link: https://github.com/CasualOffice/opencanvas

Write "OpenCanvas" as one word with a capital C. Describe it as open source under Apache 2.0. Avoid wording that suggests a partnership or endorsement unless the project has agreed to it in writing, and don't use the OpenCanvas name or logo as your product's own.

What the licence asks

OpenCanvas is open source under the Apache License 2.0 (LICENSE), copyright CasualOffice. In short:

  • you may use, modify, self-host, embed and redistribute it, in open or closed, commercial or non-commercial products and services;
  • when you redistribute it, include the LICENSE and the NOTICE file (Apache-2.0 §4(d)), and mark files you changed;
  • the licence grants no rights to the OpenCanvas name or logo beyond describing where your product comes from.

The LICENSE file is the authority; this summary is not legal advice.

What the project asks (voluntary)

If OpenCanvas helps you, please say so: in an application footer, an About dialog, your documentation or your third-party notices. This is a request, not a condition. OpenCanvas never adds attribution to your application or to the files it saves.

Accessibility

  • A logo used as attribution needs a text alternative: alt="Powered by OpenCanvas" on the image, or visible text beside a decorative image with alt="".
  • Use the light-background logo on light surfaces and the dark-background logo on dark ones, so the wordmark keeps its contrast.
