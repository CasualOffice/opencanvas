Roadmap

Where OpenCanvas stands and what comes next, generated from the project's roadmap and its execution tracker. Every unit of work has a row there; this page shows each row's status as the tracker records it.

This is the project's plan, not a commitment. No dates are promised for what is not released. "Built, in review" means the work is merged and verified and waits for the owner's approval; "Backlog" means agreed but not scheduled. Ideas and requests are welcome as GitHub issues.

Release roadmap

OpenCanvas integrates with any third-party application through standardized APIs and SDKs, without depending on specific CasualOffice products. Hosts connect through the documented launch tokens, callbacks, WOPI, REST, postMessage, the <open-canvas> SDK and MCP; nothing in the server or editor assumes a particular host. This keeps the project independent and modular, and open to a broad developer ecosystem (ADR-038).
